Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical turnaround time for a project?

Turnaround times vary depending on the scope of the project, but brand identity packages typically take between 1 to 2 weeks from initial concept to final delivery.

What is your pricing structure?

Projects are priced based on scope and requirements, offering flexible options whether you need a standalone logo, full brand identity, or ongoing design support.

How many revision rounds are included?

Every project includes dedicated revision rounds to ensure the final design aligns precisely with your vision and aesthetic goals.

What are your payment terms and how do I pay?

For custom projects, we typically require a 50% upfront deposit to begin work and the remaining 50% upon final approval. International payments are securely handled through Payoneer.

What do I need to provide before we start a project?

Just your brand vision, any existing logos, color preferences, or reference examples you like. If you don't have these yet, we can build them from scratch during the Brand Identity phase.

How will we communicate during the design process?

We keep communication transparent and structured through direct messaging and milestone updates so you are always in the loop from concept to final delivery.
